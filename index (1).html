About Campus Connect

Campus Connect brings together every club, event, and announcement at KLE Tech Hubli into a single, simple place — built so students never have to dig through five different group chats to find out what's happening. This project was built for the MIB Software Cluster Recruitment Build Challenge, Theme 3: Campus Connect.
